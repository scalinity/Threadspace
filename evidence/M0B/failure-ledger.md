Classes are the M0B failure-policy categories: implementation defect, permissions/config issue, provider limitation, macOS limitation, SPEC assumption failure. Harness defects are listed separately because they affect evidence, not the product.

## Limitations found (not failures)

- **Route latency.** Across 75 exact native routes: median 874 ms, p95 952 ms, max 964 ms. That is above the SPEC §20.2 target of p95 ≤ 750 ms, and all are within the 2 s hard budget (`latency.json`). The time is two `osascript` launches (~240 ms each) and two provider lookups (~175 ms each). Owner: M13.
- **Discovery cost.** Each `claude agents --json --all` costs ~0.14 CPU-s and a transient 146 MB process. Two calls per 5 s pass average ~5–6 % of one core, above the §20.2 idle-companion target. Owner: M13.
- **Switch detection latency.** In-place `/clear`/`/resume` reached the projection 2.1–2.9 s after the provider's own SessionStart, bounded by the 5 s pass. A route always does its own fresh lookup, so this delays display, not correctness.
